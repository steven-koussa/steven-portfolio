FEATURED CASE STUDY / BUSINESS WEBSITE

Digital Soft Net

From finding a repair to starting a conversation.

A practical front-end project for a repair business in Zouk Mosbeh, Lebanon, helping visitors explore services and prepare a repair enquiry through WhatsApp.

My role
Front-end development
Business
Phone, computer & console repairs
Built with
HTML, CSS & JavaScript

01 / THE GOAL

Help customers take the next step

A visitor with a broken device needs to know whether the business offers the right repair and how to get in touch. The goal was to bring service discovery and a useful first enquiry into one website.

02 / WHAT I BUILT

Three decisions behind the flow

  • Searchable services. Visitors can filter repair options to find a relevant service.
  • Structured enquiries. The request form prepares a WhatsApp message with the customer's contact details, device, urgency, and problem.
  • Direct contact. Phone and WhatsApp links offer a quick alternative to the form, within a responsive layout.

03 / THE DELIVERED RESULT

A live website with a clear enquiry path

Visitors can find a service, describe the repair, and open a prepared message in WhatsApp to send to the business. Explore the live demo to see the complete flow.
